## Who we are

We are Vardara Software, a boutique software development company that loves building things. Ruby on Rails is our first love, AWS is our home, and AI is our frontier.

We've been building with Ruby on Rails for over five years and have never looked back. Alongside Rails we work with React.js for rich, data-heavy interfaces, React Native for cross-platform mobile apps, and Progressive Web Apps when you want a native-like feel from a single codebase.

## What we do

We partner with startups and SMEs to design, build and run software that matters:

- **Custom software development**: Rails applications, React UIs and React Native mobile apps, from MVP to production.
- **AWS cloud deployment**: production-grade infrastructure, CI/CD pipelines, auto-scaling and cost optimization.
- **AI automation**: workflows, AI agents and integrations that take repetitive work off your team's plate. We help businesses embrace AI.

We also build our own products. [BirdView](https://github.com/remotespacex) is a SaaS for timesheet management and invoicing, and [Saasify](https://github.com/remotespacex) is an open source Ruby on Rails boilerplate that lets you focus on your product instead of scaffolding.

## Our approach

- **Agile, always.** We work in short iterations, ship often and adapt as we learn.
- **Embedded squads.** With our *Hire a Squad* model, our engineers join your team, tools and rituals as an extension of your company.
- **Quality over quantity.** We build high-quality, testable and scalable software, and we take on only the work we can do well.
